# Trend Analysis And Backtest

## Stable Pattern

- Current official structure is 100 English, 50 Reasoning and 50 combined Quant/GK.
- Synonym/antonym, grammar correction, spelling, idioms, one-word substitution, cloze and comprehension are durable English archetypes.
- Series, analogy, coding, relations, directions, Venn/syllogism and spatial reasoning are durable Reasoning archetypes.
- Percentages, ratio, profit/loss, averages, interest, work/time/speed, mensuration and DI recur as Quant archetypes.

## Recent Shift

- The recent 2023 ICRB and 2024 PRL papers use the 200-question objective structure, unlike older 90-question or two-paper formats.
- Computer knowledge moved out of the written 200-question pattern and belongs to the qualifying skill test in the 2026 advertisement.

## Weak Signal

- 2023 shows 25 Quant + 25 GK inside Part C. 2024's missing pages prevent confirming the same transition. The mocks use 25/25 because it is the strongest complete direct evidence.
- Clustering of spelling, idiom, cloze or visual reasoning varies between 2023 and 2024; mocks rotate these clusters but do not predict placement.

## Obsolete Pattern

- Older descriptive sections, +3/-1 marking, two-paper formats and written computer-literacy questions are obsolete for the 2026 written mock structure.

## Backtest

A forecast made only from pre-2023 papers would have over-weighted older English/GK/arithmetic/computer segmentation and under-predicted the 100-question English block, 50-question Reasoning block and combined Part C. It would still have anticipated durable archetypes: vocabulary, grammar, arithmetic percentages/ratio/profit, series, analogy and relations. Therefore the mocks weight current structure from 2026/2023 heavily and use older papers only for concept durability.
