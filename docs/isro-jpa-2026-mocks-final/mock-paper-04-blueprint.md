# Mock Paper 04 Blueprint

This is an evidence-informed hard practice paper for ISRO JPA 2026. It is not official and not a prediction.

## Structure

- English Language and Comprehension: 100 questions
- General Intelligence and Reasoning: 50 questions
- Quantitative Aptitude and General Knowledge: 50 questions
- Total: 200 questions, 200 marks
- Duration: 150 minutes
- Negative marking: 0.25 mark per incorrect answer

## Quant/GK Allocation

Paper 04 uses 25 Quantitative Aptitude and 25 General Knowledge questions. This follows the complete 2023 ICRB observation where Part C visibly splits into 25 Quant and 25 GK. The 2026 advertisement does not prescribe a fixed subdivision, and the 2024 PRL source is incomplete around the transition.

## Topic Mix

English follows the observed current-paper mix: vocabulary and usage, grammar/error detection, idioms, one-word substitutions, spelling, fillers, voice/narration, sentence ordering, cloze and comprehension. Reasoning covers series, analogy, classification, coding, directions, ranking, arrangements, syllogisms, Venn logic, calendar/clock and spatial reasoning. Quant covers arithmetic, commercial arithmetic, work/time/speed, algebra, geometry, mensuration, trigonometry, statistics and DI. GK balances current affairs with history, geography, culture, science, polity and economy.

## Difficulty Design

Difficulty is created through precise English usage, close distractors, multi-step reasoning, applied arithmetic and compact time pressure. Obscure trivia and disputed current facts are avoided.

## Evidence

- Official 2026 advertisement Annexure III and syllabus
- Complete 2023 ICRB JPA paper as strongest direct structural precedent
- 2024 PRL JPA as secondary evidence with source-page gaps noted
- 2019 and older papers only for durable concepts and archetypes
