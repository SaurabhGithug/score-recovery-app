# Master Exam Blueprint

These five papers are evidence-informed hard practice papers for ISRO JPA 2026, not official or predicted papers.

The controlling structure is the official 2026 scheme: English 100, General Intelligence and Reasoning 50, Quantitative Aptitude and General Knowledge combined 50, total 200 questions, 200 marks, 150 minutes, and 0.25 negative marking. Each paper uses a 25 Quant + 25 GK split because the complete 2023 ICRB paper visibly uses that allocation; this is an evidence-informed choice, not an official sub-quota.

The final paper-setting label used here is the Observed ISRO JPA Examination Fingerprint: current 100/50/50 objective structure; English dominated by grammar, vocabulary, idioms, spelling, usage, cloze and comprehension; Reasoning with series, analogy, coding, arrangements, Venn/syllogism, directions and spatial items; Part C with arithmetic, commercial arithmetic, DI and balanced GK.
