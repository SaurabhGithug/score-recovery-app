# IES Master + Made Easy test calendar, 3 Oct to 1 Nov 2026

> **Superseded (4 Oct 2026).** Follow `docs/pyq-plan.md` and `docs/made-easy-plan.md`, which are the plan in the app's calendar. This early draft splits IES reasoning tests into two sprints, but IES gives one attempt per test, and its mock list is out of date.

Built 2 Oct 2026. Exam window: **2-6 Nov 2026 (proposed by SSC; shift and centre come on the admit card)**. Priority: **raise Technical (Civil) marks**; three timed Reasoning blocks and a 30-minute hot-fact drill every non-mock day; GK / Current Affairs a few times a week. Typical non-mock day: about 5-6 hours including review, so trim the sectional tests first if you have less.

## Rules this plan follows
- **13 full mocks**: 6 app past papers (2024, 2023) + 7 Made Easy full tests (ME-FLT-8 kept as spare). Mock days: 120 min mock + ~120 min analysis, no IES topic test.
- **Technical practice** uses IES Civil topic tests, hottest first (ranking below), so the weakest, most-asked topics get done before the mocks pick up the same topics. Made Easy's eight 50-question technical subject tests are used as *unseen checkpoints* right after the matching IES block, giving a 50-mark technical score you can track.
- **Hot-fact drill (new)**: 30 min every day. It repeats the facts from the IES blocks you did 1, 3, 7 and 14 days ago (spaced repetition), using the recurring fact families in `docs/hot-fact-families.md` (315 facts that the exam asked in 2+ cycles). Options must be shuffled so you learn the fact, not the position. On mock days the drill is the mock's error log.
- **Reasoning** is now **three timed blocks a day** on non-mock days: one full 50-question set (30 min) plus two 25-question sprints (12 min each) taken from IES Reasoning papers 2018-2022, and Made Easy's four reasoning tests weekly. Ladder and rules below the table. **GK / CA**: 8 sessions (4 Made Easy GK tests, 4 IES GA papers). IES GA papers are 2022 or older, so **fresh Current Affairs (last 6-12 months) must come from another source**.
- **Contamination guard**: IES GS papers are from 2016-2022 and the mocks are 2023-24 papers; IES's 'previous 20 years' Civil tests may still contain 2023-24 questions (the page doesn't say). Do topic tests *before* the mock that covers the same topic, and trust Made Easy mocks most for score tracking.
- **Flex days** (Oct 16-26 technical blocks): after each mock, replace a block with the weakest topic in your error log. The default order below is only the starting guess.
- Big tests: T42 (197 min), T37 (217 min), T36 (146 min) are placed on weekend days. **Check whether the IES portal lets you pause**; if not, do them in one sitting.

## Reasoning ladder (your target: 45 / 50; planning floor: 40)
Score = correct - 0.25 x wrong. **45 needs all 50 attempted at 92% accuracy or better (about 30 s per question).** Your starting point (about 30 attempted at 80-90%) is about 24 marks.

| Week | Attempt at least | Accuracy at least | Score it gives |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 (Oct 3-9) | 38 | 90% | about 32 |
| 2 (Oct 10-16) | 42 | 90% | about 36 |
| 3 (Oct 17-23) | 46 | 90% | about 40 |
| 4 (Oct 24-Nov 1) | 48-50 | 92% | about 43-45 |

Log per question: time taken, type, right/wrong. Rules: skip at 45 s in weeks 1-2, 40 s in week 3, 35 s in week 4; never leave a question blank in the last 2 minutes (at -0.25 a guess is worth it).

Rest-of-window: **Nov 2-6 exam**. Suggested: Nov 1 light revision only, sleep early.
