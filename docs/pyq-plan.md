# PYQ plan, 3 Oct to 1 Nov 2026 (previous-year questions only)

Scope: **only previous-year questions**: IES Civil topic tests, IES Reasoning and GA past-paper sets, and a daily hot-fact drill from the app's recurring fact families. Full-length mocks and Made Easy subject tests are planned separately next and will sit on top of this.

## How it works
1. **Hottest topics first.** IES Civil tests are ranked by how often their syllabus topic appeared in the last 12 papers (2022-24). Tier 1 first, then 2, then 3. Tier 4 (rare topics) and the 19 tests outside the notice's syllabus are skipped.
2. **Attempt, then review.** Do each test on the IES portal (Pass 1), then read the solution of every wrong, guessed or slow question (about 50% extra time). Log: score, minutes, accuracy, topic.
3. **Every hot test three times.** Each Tier 1 and Tier 2 test (36 tests) gets Pass 2 and Pass 3 on later days (see below). Tier 3 tests are done once; their wrong questions go into the hot-fact drill.
4. **Daily hot-fact drill, 30 min.** Facts from blocks done 1, 3, 7 and 14 days ago, options shuffled (spaced repetition). Source list: `docs/hot-fact-families.md`.
5. **Reasoning three times a day**: one full 50-question set (30 min) and two 25-question sprints (12 min each). Ladder: attempt 38 / 42 / 46 / 48-50 by week at 90%+ accuracy. Target 45 needs all 50 at 92%+; planning floor 40.
6. **GK / CA** on Tue, Thu and Sat (IES GA sets are 2022 or older, so add 20 min of fresh current affairs from another source).
7. **Last 5 days (Oct 28-Nov 1): no new tests.** Oct 29-Nov 1 hold the Pass 3 items; Oct 28 has no pass due, so use it to catch up on any pass you missed.

## How the three passes work
Goal: solve every hot test (Tier 1 and 2, 36 tests) at least three times.
- **Pass 1: the full test** on its scheduled day (the Civil column below), then review every wrong, guessed or slow question.
- **Pass 2: about 30% of the test time** on the first review day after Pass 1 (Oct 7, Oct 14, Oct 21, Oct 28).
- **Pass 3: about 20% of the test time** on one of Oct 29, Oct 30, Oct 31, Nov 1, biggest tests spread first so the four days carry about the same load.
- **How to do Pass 2 and 3:** re-sit the test if the portal allows it; otherwise open its solutions and re-solve your wrong and guessed questions without looking. At least 5 min per pass.
- **Tier 3 tests (24) get no extra passes:** their wrong questions go into the daily hot-topic drill instead.
