## How it works
1. **Hottest topics first.** IES Civil tests are ranked by how often their syllabus topic appeared in the last 12 papers (2022-24). Tier 1 first, then 2, then 3. Tier 4 (rare topics) and the 19 tests outside the notice's syllabus are skipped.
2. **Attempt, then review.** Do each IES test once (Pass 1), then read the solution of every wrong, guessed or slow question (about 50% extra time, not counted in the minutes below).
3. **Every hot test three times.** Each Tier 1 and Tier 2 test (36 tests) gets Pass 2 and Pass 3 on later review days. Tier 3 tests are done once; their facts come back in the daily drill.
4. **10 full mocks** (9 plus one optional): all 8 Made Easy Full Syllabus Tests and two unseen real 2024 papers in this app, every 2-4 days. **No IES topic tests on a mock day**: the mock, its analysis, a reasoning speed set and review, and a shorter drill.
5. **Made Easy subject tests** you have not done yet come right after the matching IES block (unseen checks); the three GK tests replace IES GA sets on their days.
6. **Hot-topic drill every day** (30 min; 20 min on mock days): the facts from your topic tests of 1, 3, 7 and 14 days ago, options shuffled.
7. **Reasoning three times a day**: (1) a full 50-question IES set in 30 min (on mock days, the mock's reasoning section), (2) a second, different IES set in one sitting in 25 min for speed, (3) a 10-minute review of the day's wrong, guessed and slow questions from the IES Report. IES gives **one attempt per test**, so every reasoning test is used exactly once. Ladder: attempt 38 / 42 / 46 / 48-50 by week at 90%+ accuracy; 45 needs all 50 at 92%+.
8. **Last days:** 28 Oct, 30 Oct and 1 Nov are review days (Pass 2/3, drills; also Wed 28 Oct: Made Easy subject test 15; Fri 30 Oct: Made Easy subject test 16); 29 Oct is Made Easy Full Syllabus Test 8; 31 Oct's mock is optional (only if your shift is on 4-6 Nov).
9. **One evening a week off.** If a day spills over, move the leftover to the next review day (7, 14, 21, 28 Oct).
10. **Re-planned on 5 Oct.** 3 and 4 Oct keep only what was taken on IES; T42 and T37 (the two most-asked topics) moved to the weekend of 10-11 Oct, and 7 and 21 Oct, review days with room, also take a few new tests.

## The three passes
- **Pass 2: about 30% of the test time** on the first review day after Pass 1 (Wed 07 Oct, Wed 14 Oct, Wed 21 Oct, Wed 28 Oct).
- **Pass 3: about 20% of the test time** on one of Wed 28 Oct, Fri 30 Oct, Sun 01 Nov (not on a mock day), biggest tests spread first.
- **How:** IES gives one attempt per test (after you submit, Attempt Now becomes Report). So Pass 2 and Pass 3 are not new attempts: press Report on the test and re-solve your wrong and guessed questions without looking.
