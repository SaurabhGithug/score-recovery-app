Some sit beside a hot topic, so their questions may be filed under the neighbour (Mohr's circle beside soil shear strength). Study them together.

## 5. Caveats

- **Labels are AI-made and only spot-checked.** In the check of 255 fact families, 164 held together, 72 held partly and 19 did not. Topic tags exist for only 845 of the 1,200 recent questions.
- **Image-only older questions are missing.** Only about 64% of earlier (pre-2022) Civil questions are searchable text (1,537 of 2,400). Fact families use only those, so older repeats are undercounted.
- **This ranks what was asked, not what will be asked.** It is 12 papers from 3 cycles. A topic with under 10 questions can move a lot between cycles.
