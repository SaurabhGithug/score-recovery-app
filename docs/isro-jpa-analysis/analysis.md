# ISRO JPA previous papers: source audit and 2026 syllabus comparison

**Audit date:** 19 September 2026. **Scope:** the 11 PDFs in this workspace, inspected page by page. This is an analysis of observed papers, not a claim about how ISRO designs future questions. No mock papers were generated.

## Primary finding for the 2026 exam

The controlling document is [2026 ICRB advertisement, Annexure III](https://www.isro.gov.in/media_isro/pdf/recruitmentNotice/2026/July/Bilingual%20Advertisement_Asst_JPA_UDC_Steno_ICRB_2026_27072026.pdf#page=22): **200 objective questions, 200 marks, 150 minutes**, with +1/−0.25 marking. Part A is 100 English Language & Comprehension questions; Part B is 50 General Intelligence & Reasoning questions; Part C is 50 Quantitative Aptitude & General Knowledge questions combined. The earlier 120-minute statement in the starting brief came from the 2023/2024 papers, not the 2026 rule. The advertisement gives no fixed Quant/GK subdivision within Part C and no subtopic quotas. The [recruitment page](https://www.isro.gov.in/ICRB_Recruitment11.html) lists the CBT for 21 September 2026.

## What was extracted and how to use it

All 243 PDF pages were processed. [OCR page transcripts](ocr-pages/) preserve every page's machine-readable text, including both column crops for the 2023 and 2016 scans and the embedded text layer for the PRL files. [Question blocks](question-blocks.jsonl) contain one record per advertised objective question and separate descriptive tasks. [Question locations](question-locations.csv) give paper, question number, section, source PDF page and extraction status. [OCR quality metrics](ocr-quality.csv) give selected-body word confidence. The original PDFs remain the authority for exact symbols, diagrams, answer options and dates. `numbered_block_unverified` means the question was separated by its printed number but its wording has **not** been certified as a faithful transcription.

Across the 11 papers there are **1,210 nominal objective questions**. **1,183 are physically present** in these files; 27 are lost with omitted PRL source pages. The extraction contains **1,179 numbered text blocks**, [image pages for three OCR-resistant figure questions](visual-pages/), and page context for the partial 2019 Q88. The corpus also indexes 15 top-level descriptive tasks from older formats. These counts measure extraction coverage, not correctness of every character.

### Source defects that change the evidence

* **2024 PRL:** PDF page 9 ends at Q52 and has printed footer 7; PDF page 10 starts at Q63 with printed footer 10. Printed pages 8–9 and Q53–62 are absent. PDF page 31 ends at Q173 with printed footer 31; PDF page 32 starts Q186 with printed footer 34. Printed pages 32–33 and Q174–185 are absent. Its answer-key numbering confirms that the intended paper reached Q200, but the absent question text cannot be recovered from this PDF.
* **2019 PRL:** PDF pages 1–10 are repeated as pages 11–20. PDF page 35 has printed footer 25 and Q80–82; PDF page 36 has footer 28 and the continuation of Q88 plus Q89–90. Printed pages 26–27 are absent, so Q83–87 and Q88's initial stem/options are unavailable.
* **2023 ICRB:** all Q1–200 are present. Some are pictorial, so an OCR transcript alone cannot represent the full item. Examples: Q104–105 Venn diagrams, Q109–110 dice/box, Q115–119 figure or image questions, Q128–129 figure series, Q136–138 missing-number diagrams, Q148–149 diagrams, and Q173–175 table data.

The 2026 advertisement's indicative syllabus is broader than either recent paper's visible coverage. Trigonometry, boats/streams, pipes/cisterns, coordinate geometry and statistics are listed officially but have little or no clear representation in the visible recent questions. ## Question types, difficulty and rotation

**Question forms.** Both recent papers use four-option objective items, with mostly short independent prompts. The 2023 paper also contains a five-question comprehension set (Q96–100), visual reasoning and a three-question table set (Q173–175). The 2024 paper visibly contains sentence arrangement (Q63), a three-question reading passage (Q70–72), a ten-blank cloze set (Q87–96), Venn/figure reasoning (Q134–135, Q150), and table DI (Q164). Therefore five mocks should cover both discrete and grouped items, but the exact counts of cloze, passage, DI or pictorial items are not prescribed by Annexure III.

**Observed difficulty, not psychometric difficulty.** Direct one-step recall/recognition appears repeatedly: 2023 Q34–39, Q40–54, Q176–200; 2024 Q1–11, Q40–52 and many Q186–200. Multi-step work appears in 2023 Q153, Q156, Q165, Q168–175 and 2024 Q154–173; diagram interpretation can slow Q109–119 and Q134–150. On item structure alone, the collection spans easy through moderately demanding questions with some slower numerical/visual clusters. There are no candidate response statistics, calibrated item parameters or verified cutoff-linked difficulty data in these PDFs. Calling the whole exam “hard” would be unsupported.

**Repeated concepts, not proof of repeated questions.** Synonym/antonym and idiom questions recur across 2016 Q1–8, 2019 Q1–4 and Q16–23, 2023 Q1–20 and Q55–65, and observed 2024 Q3–11 and Q64–74. Grammar/error correction recurs in 2019 Q9–12, 2023 Q21–33 and Q66–95, and 2024 Q18–27 and Q75–86. Series, analogy, coding and visual relations appear in 2023 Q101–150 and 2024 Q101–150, with smaller older reasoning sections. Percentages, ratios, profit/loss, work and averages recur in 2012 Q47–69, 2023 Q151–172 and 2024 Q151–173. These are shared **archetypes**; the audit did not establish verbatim question reuse or a deliberate ISRO repetition method.

**Topic rotation visible between two recent papers.** 2023 has a clearly identifiable 15-question spelling run (Q40–54) and 11-question idiom run (Q55–65). In the available 2024 pages, English includes a cloze run (Q87–96), sentence ordering (Q63) and contextual vocabulary; Q53–62 are absent, so the full 2024 English mix is unknown. The observed 2023 Quant/GK order is Q151–175/Q176–200; 2024's absent Q174–185 prevent confirming its exact transition. This is a two-paper comparison, not a time series from which to forecast 2026 subtopic counts.

**Outdated structure and facts.** The 2019/2016/2012 90-objective-plus-descriptive format, 2006–2010 two-paper format, +3/−1 marking and written computer-literacy sections are obsolete as written-test templates for 2026. Current affairs in the papers are date-bound (for example 2019 Q34 Nobel Physics, 2023 Q176 lithium discovery/Q187 convention centre, 2024 Q189 chess/Q192 World Bank president). Such items show the *type* of GK prompt but their historical answers should not be transplanted as 2026 current affairs. Computer literacy now appears in the 2026 advertisement as a **qualifying skill test**, not one of Annexure III's 200 written questions.

## OCR quality and use limits

English-body Tesseract median word confidence ranges from 92.2 to 96.2 across the papers; the fraction of words below confidence 60 ranges from 5.9% to 30.4% ([metrics](ocr-quality.csv)). These are OCR engine scores, **not** probabilities that a question or answer is correct. Bilingual PRL pages lower aggregate scores. The most consequential errors were found by checking rendered PDF pages directly: 2023 Q101's scanned series reads `5, 13, 25, 41, 61`, while one OCR block reads `5, 18, 25, 41, 61`; 2024 Q151's fraction expression and Q172's radicals are mangled by the text layer/OCR; 2006 Paper II Q62's fraction symbols are also lost. English words in clean scans are often usable for search, but math notation, Hindi, tables and figures require image review before reuse. The `question-blocks.jsonl` file is an **audit aid**, not an answer-key-ready transcription.

## Reliability verdict for five hard 200-question mocks

**Yes, with defined limits.** The official 2026 Annexure III supplies the controlling 100/50/50, 200-mark, 150-minute blueprint. A complete 2023 ICRB JPA/Steno paper and 178 visible questions from the structurally similar 2024 PRL JPA paper provide enough item archetypes and section coverage to write **five original** 200-question practice papers at a deliberately hard level. Use the 2026 syllabus to include under-sampled listed topics, use fresh date-checked GK, and validate every new answer, diagram and numerical expression. “Hard” would be a practice design choice; this collection does not measure or predict 2026 exam difficulty. The older PDFs should influence recurring concepts only, and the missing PRL pages should never be filled by inference.
