# Recurring fact families in SSC JE Paper-I Civil (2017-2024)

Built 2 Oct 2026 from the 2,519 public SSC Civil questions in the app (Mar 2017 to Jun 2024; image-only questions are not included). Each line is **one fact that the exam asked in at least two different exam cycles**, possibly worded differently. Family names were written by an AI from the question text and spot-checked by a second model: of 255 families checked, **164 held together, 72 partly (one member did not belong), 19 did not**. Treat each line as a pointer to what to drill, not as a verified fact. No answers are stated here; the answer keys come from the app's own question bank.

**315 recurring families** out of 1778; the rest were asked only once in the searchable papers.
